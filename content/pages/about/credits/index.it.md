TinyTank tiene insieme librerie collaudate: i componenti [Symfony](https://symfony.com),
[Twig](https://twig.symfony.com), [league/commonmark](https://commonmark.thephpleague.com),
[Datastar](https://data-star.dev), [Vite](https://vite.dev), [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com),
il font [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) e le icone [Lucide](https://lucide.dev).
